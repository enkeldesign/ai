# Climate + AI mechanics

> This file separates established mechanisms, plausible extrapolation, and speculative story mechanisms. It is a research map, not yet a sourced dossier.

## Evidence tags

- **SUPPORTED** — mechanism is well established in current engineering/science.
- **PLAUSIBLE** — reasonable near-future extrapolation, needs sourcing and parameter checks.
- **SPECULATIVE** — usable only with explicit uncertainty and a strong borderland remainder.

## Compute infrastructure cascade

### Electricity demand — SUPPORTED

Large compute facilities can impose substantial new electrical loads. In the novel, the important mechanism is not simply carbon emissions; it is **priority conflict**. Compute loads compete with residential demand, industry, transport electrification, cooling, hospitals, and grid reserves during extreme conditions.

Story consequences:
- emergency curtailment decisions;
- fossil peaker plants kept online or restarted;
- grid upgrades redirected toward compute corridors;
- local power quality and reliability conflicts;
- political decisions about who gets uninterrupted electricity.

### Water demand — SUPPORTED

Data-center cooling and electricity generation can both consume water. The novel can exploit the mismatch between globally abstract cloud services and locally concrete watersheds.

Story consequences:
- aquifer drawdown disputes;
- cooling restrictions during drought;
- municipal rationing while compute campuses retain contracted access;
- hot-water discharge or altered thermal regimes;
- conflict over potable vs reclaimed water.

### Waste heat — SUPPORTED locally / PLAUSIBLE at story scale

Compute ultimately becomes heat. Facilities reject this heat to air or water. Local heat-island and plume effects are physically real, but any claim that data-center waste heat directly drives rapid global warming would be too strong.

Use waste heat as:
- a local microclimate amplifier;
- a nocturnal temperature stressor;
- a contributor to fog, plume behavior, or boundary-layer oddities under specific conditions;
- an energy source that becomes politically valuable or dangerous when heat-reuse systems fail.

### Construction and supply chain — SUPPORTED

Rapid compute buildout drives cement, steel, copper, transformers, generators, chips, batteries, cooling equipment, roads, and substations.

Story consequences:
- particulate pollution;
- diesel backup fleets;
- transformer shortages;
- mining and refining expansion;
- rushed environmental permitting;
- industrial accidents hidden inside an AI boom narrative.

## Blood rain mechanism

Status: **PLAUSIBLE, RESEARCH REQUIRED**

Working causal chain:

1. A large regional fire/industrial episode injects fine mineral and combustion particles into the atmosphere.
2. Iron-bearing dust from disturbed soil, steel/cement activity, industrial emissions, or an atmospheric intervention becomes oxidized.
3. Hygroscopic particles act as condensation nuclei or are scavenged by precipitation.
4. Rain falls visibly red/rust-colored over a geographically striking area.
5. Analysis identifies iron oxides plus combustion products and industrial tracers.

### Borderland remainder

The technical explanation should explain the color but not everything. Candidate remainders:

- the rainfall boundary matches an administratively meaningful region too precisely;
- the event begins minutes after a system warning that was never publicly issued;
- isotopic/trace signatures imply sources too geographically separated to have mixed on the measured timescale;
- the AI predicts street-level onset with implausible accuracy;
- samples contain an engineered particle morphology whose official purpose is unclear.

Do not use all of these. One strong remainder is better than five weak mysteries.

## Atmospheric engineering tie-in

Status: **SPECULATIVE / HIGH SCRUTINY**

Potential story mechanism: a compute-linked climate intervention program uses aerosol or cloud-modification infrastructure to protect grid/compute regions from heat or to increase regional precipitation. Industrial particulates unintentionally or deliberately interact with this intervention.

Requirements before canonization:
- define particle chemistry;
- define delivery mechanism;
- define spatial scale;
- model plausible transport time;
- establish why conventional monitoring misses or misclassifies it;
- establish incentives for secrecy.

## Failure-loop model

The strongest climate/AI mechanism is a feedback loop rather than one giant causal claim:

**More AI deployment → more compute infrastructure → more power/water/material pressure → more climate adaptation and social triage → greater dependence on AI optimization → more AI deployment.**

The loop becomes apocalyptic when extreme weather removes slack from systems that were already optimized close to their limits.

## Research backlog

- Current hyperscale electricity-demand ranges and projections.
- Cooling-system water use by design and climate.
- Measured local heat effects around very large facilities.
- Red rain / blood rain historical cases and particle chemistry.
- Iron oxide aerosol transport and wet deposition.
- Cloud seeding and emerging solar-radiation-management proposals.
- Grid emergency load-shedding protocols.
- Generator emissions from large backup fleets.
- How AI-controlled grids and water systems are currently architected.
