## 2026-10-07 — Revelation mappings stay implicit

**Decision:** The production team should maintain a coherent underlying Revelation mapping, but the manuscript should generally not name or explain those correspondences for the reader. Recognition should be available rather than required.

**Why:** Erik wants readers to connect dots, disagree, and discuss the mapping rather than be told the answer. The biblical structure is substrate, not an allegory key printed into the prose.

**Consequences:** Characters may notice or debate apocalyptic patterns, but the narrator should not routinely certify mappings such as `white rider = tech leader` or `Beast = X`.

**Owner:** Erik

## 2026-10-07 — Architecture before chapter optimization

**Decision:** Whole-book disposition and story epics take priority over further prologue polishing. Use an epics-before-features hierarchy: architecture → epics → chapters → scenes → line polish.

**Why:** The project deep-dived into the prologue before establishing the macro story. The prologue was useful as a prototype, but local optimization should not determine the novel's structure.

**Consequences:** The blood-rain prologue remains provisional. `docs/story-architecture.md` is now the active planning artifact, and chapter drafting requires a parent epic and a clear chapter function unless explicitly labeled exploratory prototype.

**Owner:** Erik + SOL

## 2026-10-07 — Current working Revelation hypotheses

These are **working hypotheses, not canon**:

- The charismatic tech leader is a strong candidate for the Seal 1 / white-rider function and may later become recognizable as an aspect of the Beast.
- Seal 3 / black rider likely maps to ordinary economic scarcity and allocation amid AI-driven labor displacement and institutional failure, without relying on speculative implant/credit technology.
- Uploading as a "rapture" analogue is potentially useful, but belongs to broader Christian end-times imagery rather than being treated as a direct scene from Revelation.
- Blood rain remains a viable borderland event, but its exact place in the Revelation structure remains open.

**Owner:** Erik + team, pending architecture review
