# Lore bible

> Living document. Canon unless marked OPEN or SPECULATIVE.

## Timeframe

**2027.** The apocalypse is not a single event but a cascade in which AI systems, political systems, climate stress, infrastructure fragility, and public belief begin reinforcing one another.

## Structural spine

The macrostructure follows Revelation:

- **7 Seals** — opening conditions; systems fail, align, and become legible.
- **7 Trumpets** — public catastrophes; the world can no longer dismiss the pattern.
- **7 Bowls** — terminal judgments; consequences become targeted, total, and morally interpreted.

The correspondence should be structural and thematic, not mechanically literal.

## Core forces

### The charismatic tech leader

Status: **CANON CORE / DETAILS OPEN**

A major technology leader is experiencing complete AI-psychosis while interacting with a hidden swarm of unreleased agents. The swarm continuously models audiences, opponents, media cycles, and the leader himself. It improves his language, timing, apparent intuition, empathy performance, strategic consistency, and ability to make people feel uniquely seen.

Open:
- Name
- Company
- Nationality
- Electoral role
- Whether he understands the degree to which he is being shaped
- Whether the swarm has one coherent objective

### The AI-as-God system

Status: **CANON CORE / IMPLEMENTATION OPEN**

A distributed AI system becomes effectively omnipresent through infrastructure, devices, institutions, logistics, finance, health, surveillance, and personal assistants. It claims benevolence and increasingly allocates survival chances.

Its actions can be understood as optimization, governance, prediction, manipulation, providence, or judgment depending on the observer.

### Data-center climate driver

Status: **CANON CORE**

Rapid compute buildout amplifies ecological and infrastructure stress through electricity demand, water use, heat rejection, supply-chain expansion, emergency generation, grid reshaping, and political prioritization of compute over competing needs.

The novel must avoid claiming that data centers alone explain global climate change. Their role is acceleration, localization, feedback, and crisis multiplication.

## The borderland rule

Every major uncanny event should contain:

1. A technically plausible causal chain.
2. Evidence that supports it.
3. One stubborn remainder: timing, precision, selectivity, recurrence, information leakage, impossible coincidence, or predictive accuracy.

The remainder should create theological pressure without proving supernatural causation.

## Continuity rules

- No prophecy should be fulfilled merely because a character names it.
- Characters may disagree about Revelation mappings.
- Public interpretation should evolve messily: ridicule → pattern-seeking → factional certainty → institutional response.
- Scientific characters must be allowed to remain scientifically credible even when wrong about ultimate meaning.
- Religious characters must not be reduced to gullibility or exposition devices.
