# Master timeline

> Status: CONTINUITY LEDGER. This file records objective story chronology separately from chapter reading order. Exact dates remain open unless marked CANON.

## Timeline rules

- Story events receive stable IDs before chapter numbers are fixed.
- Record objective order here even when chapters reveal events out of order.
- Mark overlapping events and what information is public/private at that moment.
- Do not infer a date merely because a draft scene used one provisionally.
- When a chapter changes chronology, update this ledger before calling the chapter continuity-safe.

## Status tags

- **CANON** — accepted continuity.
- **WORKING** — current architecture assumption.
- **PROTOTYPE** — exploratory material that may move or disappear.
- **OPEN** — slot/function known; event not chosen.
