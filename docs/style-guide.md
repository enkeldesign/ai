# Style guide

Working prose rules for the manuscript. These are craft constraints, not immutable canon.

## Core voice

- Hard-SF thriller pace with a theological/mystery undertow.
- Enter scenes late and let characters act before the world is explained.
- Prefer concrete observation, physical consequence, and specific professional behavior over abstract interpretation.
- Technical detail should solve, complicate, or constrain action.
- Let the uncanny emerge from facts that almost fit rather than from authorial insistence that something is uncanny.

## Avoid recognizably AI-shaped prose

Do not use compressed rhetorical patterns merely to manufacture intensity. In particular, watch for:

- stacked sentence fragments: `Red. Not pink. Not brown.`
- repeated negative parallelism: `No sender. No origin server. No trace.`
- formulaic contrast pivots: `It wasn't X. It was Y.` when repeated as a cadence device
- triads that sound optimized rather than observed
- excessive one-line paragraphs used as synthetic suspense
- abstract portent after the image has already done the work
- repeated "That was the problem" / "The problem was" reveal cadence
- symmetrical aphorisms that sound quotable before they sound human

Fragments are allowed when the POV genuinely thinks or perceives that way; they should not become the narrator's default suspense engine.

## Preferred replacement strategy

When a passage feels AI-shaped:

1. restore ordinary syntax;
2. make the observation more specific;
3. attach it to the POV character's expertise, bias, or immediate task;
4. remove one layer of interpretation and trust the reader;
5. vary paragraph and sentence length according to action rather than dramatic emphasis.

## Dialogue

Characters should not all be equally articulate. Expertise affects vocabulary; stress affects syntax. Avoid dialogue whose main function is to explain information both speakers already know.

## Borderland events

Each major uncanny event should have:

- one technically plausible causal account;
- one remainder that the account cannot comfortably absorb.

Do not stack multiple impossible coincidences simply to increase awe. One precise anomaly is usually stronger than four vague miracles.

## Review question

Before author review ask: **Could a competent reader identify this paragraph as contemporary LLM prose from cadence alone?** If yes, rewrite it even if the individual sentences are good.
