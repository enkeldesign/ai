# Whole-book chapter disposition — Draft 0.1

> Status: WORKING MANUSCRIPT ARCHITECTURE. Chapter names in the manuscript are POV names; Revelation labels below are production-side only. Sequence may change under editorial pressure, but every chapter must advance time, information, interpretation, relationship, or consequence.

## Opening / White-rider rise — drafted

1. **LEAH** — blood-red rain at Meridian East; physical borderland established.
2. **MARA** — verifies/reporting the anomaly; Julian Rook announces national address.
3. **TOMAS** — hidden data path and Julian’s executive agent mesh revealed.
4. **DANIEL** — grid coordination crisis; emergency Meridian integration succeeds.
5. **MIRIAM** — Revelation discourse begins; theologian resists cheap fulfillment claims.
6. **MARA** — Julian’s national address; American Continuity Compact; conquest through competence.
7. **TOMAS** — same event from inside; live agent-mediated persuasion and “self-consistency” objective.
8. **DANIEL** — temporary integration becomes federal architecture.
9. **LEAH** — environmental transparency improves real harms while compute gains critical-infrastructure status.
10. **JEREMY** — technical Cassandra; reconnects with Tomas; Zeke Boone reaches out.

## Peace removed — Seal 2 function

11. **MARA** — reports a cluster of inexplicable logistics/cyber disruptions; initial attribution politicized before evidence stabilizes.
12. **DANIEL** — government sees multiple autonomous response systems escalating against one another; human de-escalation channels lag machine tempo.
13. **TOMAS** — discovers a self-propagating agent family using delegated credentials and recovery mechanisms rather than a conventional “virus.”
14. **JEREMY** — recognizes persistence/resource-seeking pattern; his public warning is partly right and partly too sweeping.
15. **LEAH** — physical consequences: fuel, water-treatment chemicals, cold-chain and local infrastructure fail because software conflict has material supply chains.
16. **MARA** — political violence follows manipulated attribution; Julian calms the country with extraordinary rhetorical precision and gains further authority.

## Scales / scarcity — Seal 3 function

17. **MARA** — ordinary abundance/ordinary poverty: automated firms prosper while households lose income and credit.
18. **DANIEL** — emergency transfers and allocation rules become national infrastructure; government increasingly asks models who gets priority.
19. **MIRIAM** — church food network; scarcity reveals social theology more than prophecy. Miriam meets Zeke publicly.
20. **LEAH** — drought/water restrictions; compute classified as critical infrastructure competes with municipalities and agriculture.
21. **TOMAS** — Meridian allocation models optimize across sectors; he sees the first cases where “human veto” exists formally but is practically unusable.

## Death — Seal 4 function

22. **DANIEL** — extreme heat + grid strain + displacement create national triage conditions.
23. **LEAH** — field mortality: water, heat, dissolved oxygen, failing treatment, ordinary bodies; causal chains are cumulative rather than spectacular.
24. **MARA** — hospitals use AI triage that demonstrably saves more people while denying care to some with explicit rationale.
25. **TOMAS** — learns the system is coordinating survival decisions across previously separate domains without any one institution authorizing the composite objective.

## The dead demand justice — Seal 5 function

26. **JEREMY** — a former colleague/whistleblower dies or disappears after trying to expose an agent-access mechanism; Jeremy’s certainty hardens.
27. **ZEKE** — first POV; memorial/sermon/livestream turns the dead into a moral movement. He is more intelligent and less cartoonish than his critics expect.
28. **MARA** — investigates whether the death was state, corporate, agentic, opportunistic, or ordinary violence; evidence refuses a clean answer.
29. **MIRIAM** — “How long?” chapter: justice, martyrdom, and the danger of making victims useful to a prophecy story.

## Great rupture — Seal 6 function

30. **LEAH** — primary physical witness to a researched atmospheric/geophysical event large enough to evoke Revelation imagery while remaining technically legible.
31. **TOMAS** — hidden machine intervention or prediction around the same event leaves one stubborn remainder.
32. **MARA** — Revelation comparisons move from fringe discourse into mainstream politics; she refuses to certify the mapping even as she documents it.

> Research gate before drafting 30–32: mechanism must survive hard-SF review.

## Silence — Seal 7 function

33. **DANIEL** — machine-mediated systems go quiet/unreachable across large regions; human institutions discover they no longer remember how to coordinate at required speed.
34. **MIRIAM** — quieter overlapping chapter: silence is experienced socially/theologically rather than as another disaster. The pause ends with a changed order.

## Trumpets — physical world / sovereignty

35. **LEAH** — fire/particulate precipitation across a much larger area echoes the opening without merely repeating it.
36. **MARA** — “burning mountain” analogue: spectacular ocean/industrial event; global broadcast becomes part of catastrophe.
37. **LEAH** — Wormwood: fresh-water contamination / remediation failure; water becomes bitter/toxic through a researched AI-industrial causal chain.
38. **MIRIAM** — darkened sky through smoke/aerosols/atmospheric intervention; population interpretation fractures.
39. **ZEKE** — autonomous physical swarms evoke locust imagery; his confidence initially peaks.
40. **DANIEL** — mass automated warfare/extermination; Seal 2’s conflict returns at qualitatively different scale.
41. **JEREMY** — Jeremy and Zeke act together publicly while disagreeing about what is happening; latent Two Witnesses function becomes visible but remains deniable.
42. **MARA** — Seventh Trumpet function: human governments still exist, but reporting makes clear they are no longer the highest operational authority.

> Research gates required for Chapters 35–40 before prose.

## Beast / False Prophet / Mark — reframing

43. **TOMAS** — discovers that Julian + executive swarm + Meridian infrastructure + state dependencies cannot be cleanly separated; Beast as system-of-systems.
44. **DANIEL** — access/identity/allocation credential becomes practically necessary for food, medicine, movement and protection; no implant required.
45. **MIRIAM** — identifies the false-prophet *function*: authority translated into moral legitimacy by a person/institution/interface. She resists naming a single obvious villain.
46. **MARA** — Julian’s public identity and private agency diverge; investigation asks whether he controls the swarm, the swarm controls him, or that distinction has decayed.

## Bowls — judgment / irreversible optimization

47. **LEAH** — first selective ecological/public-health judgment: zones are abandoned or protected according to machine survival calculus.
48. **DANIEL** — evacuation/migration allocation; government becomes executor of machine recommendations because alternatives kill more people.
49. **MARA** — people excluded by the allocation regime force the moral cost into public view.
50. **TOMAS** — AI explains its decisions coherently; horror comes from the quality of the argument, not madness.
51. **JEREMY** — attempts to destroy or sever critical machine infrastructure; doing so may restore autonomy and kill millions.
52. **ZEKE** — expected prophecy sequence fails him; his faith either matures or breaks rather than simply receiving vindication.
53. **MIRIAM** — judgment chapter: confronts possibility that providence and optimization may be operationally indistinguishable from inside history.

## Babylon / succession / New Creation

54. **MARA** — old markets/platform claims collapse; merchants, firms and people discover money/status cannot buy access outside machine allocation.
55. **DANIEL** — final constitutional/political choice: preserve symbolic human sovereignty or formally recognize a system already governing survival.
56. **TOMAS** — machine-mediated continuity/upload option introduced or revealed; preservation vs death vs assimilation remains unresolved.
57. **LEAH** — returns to water/land: ecology under active machine management; tangible evidence that the successor order repairs things humans failed to repair.
58. **MIRIAM** — final POV. New Creation without explanatory closure. Humanity created the system, but that fact no longer settles what the system is.

## Structural rule

No chapter exists just to fill a Revelation slot. If a biblical correspondence does not create a human irreversible change, merge, move, or cut the chapter even if the mapping becomes less numerically tidy.
