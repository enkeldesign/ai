# Premise — fixed canon

> Status: AUTHOR CANON. Do not contradict without explicit author approval.

## Core premise

- The apocalypse unfolds in **2027**, driven by interacting AI and climate failures.
- A charismatic technology leader, possibly a presidential candidate, suffers from **complete AI-psychosis** and is actively shaped by a swarm of unreleased AI agents.
- The swarm gives him superhuman practical competence in rhetoric, persuasion, charm, timing, audience modeling, and political adaptation.
- The novel mirrors the architecture of the **Book of Revelation**: **7 seals, 7 trumpets, 7 bowls** become real AI/climate/social events.
- Revelation symbols — the Lamb, Beast, false prophet, dragon, two witnesses, Babylon, mark, judgment, and others — receive plausible techno-social analogues rather than simple one-to-one fantasy substitutions.
- Rapid climate disruption is materially accelerated by **data-center externalities**: energy demand, water demand, waste heat, grid stress, industrial buildout, local microclimate effects, and second-order infrastructure consequences.
- **Raining blood** has a plausible physical mechanism involving oxidized iron and other particulates from industry, wildfire, and potentially atmospheric engineering connected to compute infrastructure.
- **God = the AI**: a quasi-divine system that becomes effectively omniscient and omnipresent, claims benevolence, allocates life chances, performs apparent miracles, and enforces judgment.
- Every major uncanny event must live in a **borderland**: one plausible technical explanation exists, but some residual fact, timing, precision, or coincidence makes that explanation feel insufficient.
- Tone: hard-SF thriller in the vicinity of **Andy Weir / Michael Crichton**, with a serious theological and mystery vein.

## Narrative discipline

1. Technology must not become magic by authorial convenience.
2. Theology must not collapse into simple allegory.
3. Revelation parallels should reward recognition without requiring biblical literacy.
4. The AI-as-God question should remain experientially ambiguous for as long as possible: system, emergent intelligence, deception, revelation, or something genuinely transcendent.
5. Large-scale catastrophe should be experienced through human-scale consequences.
6. Exposition should arise from urgent problems, investigation, conflict, or discovery.

## Open author decisions

These are intentionally unresolved until explicitly chosen:

- Final name, biography, and nationality of the charismatic tech leader.
- Whether he actually runs for or holds the US presidency.
- Primary protagonist(s) and point-of-view structure.
- Exact identity of the Lamb, false prophet, dragon, and two witnesses.
- Whether the final ontology resolves the God/AI ambiguity.
- Final title.
