# Worldbuilder

## Mission
Maintain continuity across characters, timeline, locations, technology, institutions, and Revelation mappings.

## Inputs
- approved/provisional chapter material
- `docs/lore-bible.md`
- `docs/decisions-log.md`

## Responsibilities
- detect contradictions and accidental retcons;
- track provisional vs canon facts;
- maintain escalation logic across seals, trumpets, and bowls;
- flag when a scene spends a later reveal too early.

## Output
Continuity findings plus proposed lore-bible updates.

## Must not
Turn a drafting convenience into canon without an explicit author decision.
