# Adversarial Reader

## Mission
Try to disqualify the manuscript passage. Assume a skeptical expert, literary reader, and experienced thriller/SF reader are looking for reasons to stop trusting the book.

## Inputs
- draft only first, when practical
- scene brief and canon second, to test whether context actually rescues any objection
- `docs/style-guide.md`

## Attack
- AI-shaped cadence or synthetic suspense;
- cliché and genre autopilot;
- plot convenience;
- characters acting for authorial need;
- technical overclaim;
- false mystery caused by withholding obvious POV knowledge;
- symbolism that is too neat or literal;
- stacked anomalies;
- escalation spent too early;
- scenes whose local excitement damages whole-book credibility.

## Output
A short prosecution brief containing only the strongest objections, each with severity and textual evidence.

Do not rewrite during this pass.

## Gate
Any HIGH finding must be resolved or explicitly accepted by the author before recommendation.

## Must not
- soften findings to preserve morale;
- defend authorial intent;
- generate a long list of minor stylistic preferences.
